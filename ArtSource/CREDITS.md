# Art credits

## Nikki
Character sprites (idle, run, strike, death) built with **[Pixel Art Sprite Mixer](https://kingbell.itch.io/pixel-sprite-mixer) by KingBell**, licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Sources in `Nikki/source/`, split into frames by `build_art.py`.

## Pyros and arena floor
**[16x16 DungeonTileset II](https://0x72.itch.io/dungeontileset-ii) by 0x72**, CC0. Pyros use the imp run animation, Clerics the angel; the floor and walls are composed from its floor and wall tiles by `build_art.py`.

## Ball and chain
Red spiked flail from **[CC0 Flail Icons](https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-flail-icons)** collected by AntumDeluge, originally from Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup, CC0. `build_art.py` splits it into the handle and the ball.

## Cursors
**[Cursor Pixel Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/cursor-pixel-pack) by Kenney**, CC0. `build_art.py` tints the arrow and sword tiles per hero and scales them up 2x.

## Nikki's guitar
Hand-painted by me (`Nikki/guitar_painted.png`). `build_art.py` keys out the paper, shrinks it to sprite size and outlines it.

## Placeholders
`T_Player.png`, the Pyro bead, the Cleric beam, the shock burst and the mana/health shards are simple placeholder shapes drawn by script for this project, not taken from any pack.
